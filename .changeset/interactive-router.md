Manual routing in the layout view: X over a highlighted pad arms a route — X over a trace arms from the trace, silently (the status line alone says where X starts), and a press within the pad-snap radius of a segment end arms there, ending the needle-threading at a 0.3 mm stub tip. The walk draws clearance-checked, corridor-hinted, via-capable tracks in 0/45/90 that actually reach the pad: 0/90 prioritized (diagonals and bends cost extra), a drag-trail interaction, placed-but-unstaged components are routing obstacles, and a pad snap finishes; V drops a via (the live layer flip actually flips), double-click commits without folding the last hop onto itself, and the rubber-band preview renders from the instant the route starts — layer ink and a start dot, no invisible anchors.
